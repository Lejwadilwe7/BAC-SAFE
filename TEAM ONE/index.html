Welcome to BAC SAFE

Your trusted safety companion at Botswana Accountancy College (BAC).

Our mission is to empower every student to request emergency assistance quickly, notify campus security and emergency contacts, and share your location when needed.

Features

  • One-tap SOS activation with location sharing
  • Instant notification to Campus Security and your emergency contacts
  • Manage your trusted emergency contacts with ease
  • Real-time incident updates and status tracking
  • Dedicated security user dashboard for quick response coordination
  • Future-ready integration for wearable panic button devices

How It Works

  1. Register with your BAC student details and add emergency contacts.
  2. Login to your secure dashboard.
  3. Tap the NOT SAFE / SOS button to instantly alert security.
  4. Your location is shared upon consent; incident status is updated live.
  5. Cancel false alarms easily without delaying help if real.
